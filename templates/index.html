A secure, transparent way to vote online

Every voter is verified with a one-time password, and every ballot is stored as an encrypted ballot in a tamper-evident, hash-linked ledger.

Register to vote Login & vote

OTP-verified identity

Registration and login both require a time-bound one-time password, so only the person with access to the registered email can vote.

Blockchain vote ledger

Each vote becomes a block containing the hash of the block before it. If a recorded vote is changed, ledger validation detects it.

One voter, one vote

Once your ballot is recorded, your voter record is marked as voted and the portal won't let you cast a second one.
